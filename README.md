# Credit Card Financial & Customer Analytics Dashboard | Power BI
# Overview

An end-to-end Credit Card Analytics Dashboard developed in Power BI to analyze revenue performance, customer spending behavior, transaction trends, card portfolio performance, geographic contribution, and delinquency risk.

The project converts customer and transaction data into actionable insights that support business growth, customer retention, portfolio optimization, and risk monitoring.

Key Insights & Findings
Portfolio generated approximately $55.3M Revenue.
Total Interest Income reached $45M.
Delinquency Rate stands at 6.07%.
Texas (TX), New York (NY), and California (CA) contribute nearly 73% of total revenue.
Blue Card customers generate the highest overall revenue.
Swipe transactions account for the largest share of spending activity.
Business professionals and high-income customers are the most valuable revenue contributors.
Male customers contribute slightly higher revenue compared to female customers.
Business Recommendations
Geographic Expansion
Strengthen retention and acquisition efforts in TX, NY, and CA.
Expand marketing activities into lower-performing states.
Customer Retention
Launch loyalty programs for high-value customers.
Offer personalized rewards and spending incentives.
Card Portfolio Growth
Promote upgrades from standard cards to premium card categories.
Expand benefits for high-performing Blue Card customers.
Risk Management
Implement proactive delinquency monitoring.
Establish payment reminder and early-warning systems.
Introduce risk-based intervention programs.
Transaction Strategy
Encourage digital and contactless payments through rewards and cashback programs.
Increase engagement through targeted spending campaigns.

Executive Summary

This project analyzed $55.3M in portfolio revenue, $45M in interest income, and a 6.07% delinquency rate to evaluate customer behavior, transaction patterns, card portfolio performance, geographic contribution, and risk exposure. The analysis identified high-value customer segments, top-performing regions, and opportunities for revenue growth, customer retention, portfolio optimization, and delinquency reduction.
